Speech Module Specifics
Chunking: text is split at ~250 chars on sentence boundaries
(falls back to word boundaries).

Watchdog: if a chunk doesn't finish within an estimated time,
it forces the next chunk.

Keep-alive: every 10s, calls synth.pause() + synth.resume()
to work around Android's aggressive speech cleanup.

isSpeaking flag: the module tracks its own speaking state
because synth.speaking is unreliable on Android.

Seeking: lastIndex is updated by onboundary events. Not all
voices fire boundary events, so seek may be imprecise with some voices.
